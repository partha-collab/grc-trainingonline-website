Janette S. Levey

Employment Law Attorney

Janette S. Levey — known as "The Employer's Lawyer" — has over 20 years of legal experience, more than 10 of which she has spent focused on Employment Law.

It was during her tenure as sole in-house counsel for a mid-size staffing company headquartered in Central New Jersey, with operations across the continental United States, that she developed her passion for Employment Law.

Janette is the founder of The EmpLAWyerologist Firm, where she helps employers navigate complex workplace legal issues and avoid the litigation and workplace-safety risks that come from getting employment law wrong.
