Overview

Keep your FMLA leave records in one Excel workbook. Enter your employees and their leave once, and the tracker checks eligibility, adds up the hours, and shows who has leave left. Built for HR teams, small employers and managers.

What It Does

  • Checks each employee against the three federal FMLA eligibility tests: 12 months of service, 1,250 hours worked, and a worksite with 50 or more employees within 75 miles
  • Tracks leave against the 12-workweek entitlement for the 12-month period you choose: calendar year, fixed period, employee anniversary, or rolling backward
  • Handles continuous, intermittent and reduced-schedule leave
  • Shows hours used, hours and weeks remaining, and a clear status for every employee (OK, Low balance, Exhausted, Not eligible)
  • One-page manager summary, plus a simple PTO tracker
  • Room for up to 100 employees and 500 leave entries

Easy to Use

Works in Microsoft Excel 2010 or newer on Windows or Mac. No macros, no add-ins and no internet connection needed. Your data stays on your own computer. Most people are set up in about 10 minutes.

Who It's For

  • HR managers and HR generalists at employers with 50 or more employees
  • Small business owners and office managers who run leave without an HR system
  • Consultants and payroll teams who support clients (one license per organization)

Good to Know

  • Covers federal FMLA rules only. It does not include state or local leave laws, key-employee rules, notices or medical certifications, or the forward-measured 12-month method.
  • It is a record-keeping tool. It does not decide whether an absence qualifies for FMLA leave.
  • Built and tested for Excel. We have not tested Google Sheets and do not recommend it.
