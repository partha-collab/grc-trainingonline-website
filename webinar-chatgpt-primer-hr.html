ChatGPT: A Primer for HR Professionals (Recorded)

Recorded session
On-demand

Course Description

HR departments can now leverage AI tools like ChatGPT to streamline their processes and achieve greater efficiency. ChatGPT can be a powerful tool for HR professionals in a variety of ways, including automating repetitive tasks, providing real-time support to employees, and enhancing the overall employee experience.

A workplace run by AI is not a futuristic concept — such technology is already part of our workplaces and will continue to shape the labor market. According to a 2022 SHRM survey, nearly 1 in 4 organizations reported using AI to support HR-related activities, and 85% of employers using AI said it saves time or increases efficiency.

Why You Should Attend

Whether you (or your organization's senior leadership) are a fan of or fear artificial intelligence tools in your workplace, it's essential to know these tools' benefits and limitations and set expectations for employees' use of them.

Who Will Benefit

  • HR professionals and generalists
  • HR Managers and Directors
  • Business owners and leaders evaluating AI adoption
